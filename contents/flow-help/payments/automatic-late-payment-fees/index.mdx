## Overview

The automatic late payment fee system helps you get paid on time by automatically adding late fees to overdue invoices. Once you set it up, Flow handles everything for you - no need to manually track overdue invoices or send reminders.

## Features

- **Fully Automatic**: Late fees are added automatically every day
- **No Duplicates**: Each invoice only gets one late fee - the system prevents duplicates
- **Flexible Options**: Choose between a fixed amount or percentage-based fees
- **Grace Period**: Give clients extra time before fees apply
- **Auto-Updates**: Invoice totals and payment links update automatically
- **Clear & Transparent**: Late fees appear as separate line items on invoices

## Requirements

1. **Premium Subscription**: Late payment fees are available on Starter or Professional plans
2. **Sent Invoices**: Only applies to invoices you've already sent to clients
3. **System Active**: The automatic system runs daily in the background

## Configuration Steps

### Step 1: Navigate to Banking Settings

1. Log into your Flow account
2. Go to **Settings** → **Banking**
3. Scroll to the **Late Payment Fees** section

### Step 2: Enable Late Payment Fees

1. Select your **Fee Type**:
   - **Disabled**: Turn off late payment fees
   - **Fixed Amount**: Charge a fixed fee (e.g., £25, $50)
   - **Percentage**: Charge a percentage of the invoice total (e.g., 5%, 10%)

### Step 3: Configure Fee Amount

**For Fixed Amount:**
- Enter the amount you want to charge (e.g., `25` for £25)

**For Percentage:**
- Enter the percentage (e.g., `5` for 5%)
- Calculated based on the original invoice total (before late fees)

### Step 4: Set Grace Period (Optional)

- Enter the number of days after the due date before fees apply
- Default: `0` (fees apply immediately after due date)
- Example: `7` means fees apply 7 days after the due date

### Step 5: Save Settings

Click **Save** to apply your late payment fee configuration.

## How It Works

### Automatic Daily Process

Every day, Flow automatically:

1. **Checks Your Settings**: Looks for accounts with late payment fees enabled and configured

2. **Finds Overdue Invoices**: Identifies invoices that:
   - Have been sent to clients
   - Are unpaid
   - Are past their due date (plus any grace period you've set)
   - Don't already have a late fee

3. **Calculates the Fee**:
   - **Fixed Amount**: Uses the amount you set (e.g., £25)
   - **Percentage**: Calculates a percentage of the original invoice total

4. **Adds the Late Fee**:
   - Adds "Late Payment Fee" as a new line item on the invoice
   - Updates the invoice total automatically
   - Your client sees the updated amount immediately

5. **Updates Payment Links**: If you have Stripe enabled, payment links are automatically updated with the new total

### Example Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: Fixed Fee

**Configuration:**
- Fee Type: Fixed Amount
- Fee Amount: £25
- Grace Period: 0 days

**Invoice Details:**
- Invoice Total: £500
- Due Date: January 1, 2024
- Status: Sent, Unpaid

**Result:**
- On January 2, 2024 (the day after the due date), Flow automatically adds a £25 late fee
- New invoice total: £525
- Payment link is updated to show the new total of £525

#### Scenario 2: Percentage Fee with Grace Period

**Configuration:**
- Fee Type: Percentage
- Fee Amount: 5%
- Grace Period: 7 days

**Invoice Details:**
- Invoice Total: £1,000
- Due Date: January 1, 2024
- Status: Sent, Unpaid

**Result:**
- On January 8, 2024 (7 days after the due date), Flow automatically calculates and adds a 5% late fee
- Late fee: £1,000 × 5% = £50
- New invoice total: £1,050
- Payment link is updated to show the new total of £1,050

#### Scenario 3: Already Paid Invoice

**Invoice Details:**
- Invoice Total: £500
- Due Date: January 1, 2024
- Paid Date: January 3, 2024

**Result:**
- Flow skips this invoice because it's already been paid
- No late fee is applied

## Important Notes

### How Fees Are Calculated

- **Percentage Fees**: Calculated based on the original invoice amount (before any late fees)
- **Fixed Fees**: Uses the exact amount you set in your settings
- **One Fee Per Invoice**: Each invoice can only have one late fee applied - the system prevents duplicates

### When Fees Are Applied

- Late fees are added automatically every day
- Only applies to invoices that have been sent to clients
- Skips invoices that are already paid
- Respects your grace period setting

## Troubleshooting

### Late Fees Not Being Applied

1. **Check Your Plan**: Make sure you're on a Starter or Professional plan
2. **Verify Settings**: Go to Settings → Banking and confirm your fee type and amount are set
3. **Check Invoice Status**: Late fees only apply to:
   - Invoices you've sent to clients (not drafts)
   - Invoices that are unpaid
   - Invoices past their due date (plus your grace period)
4. **Wait a Day**: The system checks daily, so fees may appear the day after an invoice becomes overdue
5. **Contact Support**: If fees still aren't applying, contact support for assistance

### Late Fee Already Applied

- The system prevents duplicate fees
- Each invoice can only have one late fee
- If you need to adjust, manually edit the invoice

### Payment Link Not Updating

- Make sure Stripe payments are set up in your Banking settings
- Check that your Stripe account is properly connected
- Payment links should update automatically - if they don't, contact support

### Incorrect Fee Amount

**For Percentage Fees:**
- The percentage is calculated on the original invoice amount (before any late fees)
- Make sure you've entered the percentage correctly (e.g., enter `5` for 5%)

**For Fixed Fees:**
- Double-check the amount in your Banking settings
- Make sure the amount is between £5 and £200

## Best Practices

1. **Set a Grace Period**: Give clients a few days after the due date before fees apply - this shows good will and helps maintain relationships
2. **Communicate Upfront**: Let clients know about your late payment fee policy before sending invoices
3. **Choose Reasonable Amounts**: Set fees that are fair but encourage timely payment
4. **Review Regularly**: Check your settings periodically to make sure they still work for your business
5. **Start Small**: Consider testing with a small fee amount first to see how it works for you
